### Generator reach (diagnostic property in the scratch worktree, 100 cases each)
- `Instants`: the custom arbitrary is used. Years 2025/2026/2027 = 17/68/15. 26 instants fell on 28–29 Mar 2026 and 25 on 24–25 Oct 2026. Offsets were spread over Z, +01:00, +05:30, +14:00 and −05:00, and the London-midnight ±3 s branch was hit. **The DST days and period boundaries are reached.** The ±2 h windows include the spring day start (2026-03-29T00:00Z) and the autumn day start (2026-10-24T23:00Z). T-P-4 also derives the next and the last-tick ranges from each sample, so every sampled range's boundaries get checked. T-P-4 is not vacuous: four separate period mutations fail it.
- `LimitScenarios`: limit-set sizes 0/1/2/3 = 14/34/42/10, with 1,010 approvals and 883 declines. **Only 1 exact fill in 1,893 decisions.**
- `DecisionInputs`: half are frozen, and spend over a lowered limit is common (45 of 100). **0 exact fills.**
- £0 limits are reachable (`LimitCentsGen` includes `0`). But with amounts ≥ £0.01, `>` and `>=` decide the same way on a £0 limit, so this does not stand in for the exact-fill boundary.
- Custom arbitraries do not shrink: the failure output reads `Falsifiable, after 42 tests (0 shrinks)` (F-6).
