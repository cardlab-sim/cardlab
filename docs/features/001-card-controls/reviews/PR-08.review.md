# PR-08 review: Authorisation service and endpoints (processor and simulate)

## Verdict: APPROVE WITH FIXES

No blockers. 1 major (labelled `upstream:plan`, not caused by this PR's code), 3 minor (one labelled `upstream:design`),
plus nits. The service follows design §8 step for step: validate first, with no store access or lookup when invalid.
Then it resolves the card. Inside `WithCardLockAsync` it reads state, filters limits to the category, takes
`RangeContaining(request OccurredAt)` per limit, sums spend, decides, appends, and logs exactly once after the commit.
Both endpoints share one decision path (`DecideAsync`, AuthorisationService.cs:90-117) and one log call (`LogAndReturn`,
:119). Card resolution is the only difference, as DEC-14 requires. The status mapping is 200/404/400 with the same
`DecisionDto` body in every case. The PAN never reaches a response, ProblemDetails body or log line on any path I could
reach, and I probed this at Trace log level (see Evidence).
