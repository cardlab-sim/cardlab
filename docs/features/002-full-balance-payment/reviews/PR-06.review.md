## Verdict: **APPROVE WITH FIXES**

Counts: blocker 0 · major 3 · minor 3 · nits (one line at the end).

The handlers are thin and correct against the brief and design §7:
- route `{reference}` goes through `PaymentReference.TryParse`, and a bad value returns 404 "Payment not found";
- `PaymentError` maps to 409 ProblemDetails with `code`, `message` and `balanceOwed`, plus `pendingReference` and `amount` for `payment_pending`;
- `CardNotFound` returns 404 "Card not found", and a quote returns 201;
- the origin header goes through `PaymentWireNames.OriginFromHeader`;
- the steering PUT validates both fields before storing anything and merges absent fields;
- the GET defaults to `complete`/`answers` and lists the last 10 entries of the seeded `BankAccountRef`.

The handlers do no money arithmetic, hold no wording, write nothing to the store, and put no exception text or full numbers
in responses. Doc comments still match the behaviour.

The gaps are in the tests. Three realistic handler or contract mutations survive the PR's tests (F1–F3). The
time-precision mismatch the coder flagged is a real defect, but a cosmetic one (F4). It lives in `PaymentService`, not in
this PR's files.

## Answers to the coder's notes
- **Receipt time (F4):** a real defect, classified as a code fix in `PaymentService`: normalise to store precision at the source. It is not upstream. Meanwhile the 1 ms tolerance in T-I-4 is acceptable.
- **Handlers not seen RED:** mutations M2, M4, M5 and M7 are killed. M1 (receipt GET) and M3 (status merge) survive, so F1 and F2 are needed.
- **AC-34 only through `check`:** this follows DEC-13 (the GET view is pure, and the SPA triggers `check`). The "without pressing *Check status*" half belongs to T-E-3 (PR-08). Not a finding.
- **T-I-17 and null balances:** no defect.
  - `PaymentMapping.ToSnapshot` (`SqlPaymentWriter.cs:242-251`) reads `BalanceBefore/After` only for non-Pending rows, and `SettlePayment` (`:178-185`) always writes both for Completed **and** Failed (`after = before` on failure, `PaymentService.cs:497`).
  - Pending rows are legitimately null, and the seed's pending row (n = 1) is one of them; the view returns 200 with it.
  - The seed's Failed rows carrying balances matches production.
- **T-I-6's extra 409 `payment_pending` assertions:** this is a justified list change, and the only API-level pin for the W-9 body (it killed M2).
