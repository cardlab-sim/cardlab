## Verdict: **APPROVE WITH FIXES**

Counts: blocker 0 · major 2 · minor 5 · nits 4 (one line at the end).
Upstream items: F3 and F4 are `upstream:design`. They need a design decision, but they do not block the merge.

The three-step confirm follows ADR-006. Lock #1 validates, creates the Pending payment with `ConfirmCreated` and
`DebitRequested`, and marks the quote Confirmed. The bank is called outside the lock with a linked budget CTS. Lock #2
settles with `CancellationToken.None`, takes before/after from fresh sums (AC-36), and sets `EffectiveAt = DebitedAt ?? now`
(AC-34/38). An unanswered call stays Pending with `BankUnanswered` / `StatusCheckUnanswered`. Rules, arithmetic and wording
come from PR-01 classes. A Repeat records `ConfirmRepeated` and then joins or returns the stored receipt. The refusal paths
store the brief's events and never call the bank. The settle-lock-timeout path matches §9 and the PLANNER-DECISION (retry,
then best-effort `settle_lock_timeout`, then W-5). Logs carry wire names and the last four only. The view is a pure read
(DEC-13). Tests go through the ports with the fakes and assert hand-written literals (W-texts, £ amounts, event orders).

The weak spot is the in-flight join when the **first** caller goes away. The scripted probes show that a joined
repeat confirm and a concurrent status check both throw the first caller's `OperationCanceledException` (F1). Also, the
DEC-5 invariant "register before step 1 commits" is untested: a mutation that moves registration after the lock passes
the full suite (F2).
