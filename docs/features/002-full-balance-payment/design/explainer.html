CardLab · feature 002 · CL-3 slice 1 · tech design (Gate 2)

Pay the full card balance from the linked current account

A cardholder opens their card, sees what they owe, reviews one screen and confirms. Money leaves the business's current account and clears the card. Both sides always agree, and the same payment can never be sent twice or applied only halfway, even when the bank goes silent.

01 · Scope

What is being built, and why

CardLab had no idea of a balance, a current account or a payment before this slice. This design adds them, plus an external bank behind a port with one adapter: a fake bank shipped to every environment that can be told to complete, refuse or time out.

In this slice

  • Per-card balance owed = approved purchases − completed payments, floored at £0.00
  • A no-login cardholder view for each seeded card
  • One review screen, confirm, receipt (wording v1, W-1..W-10)
  • Pending / completed / failed lifecycle, exactly once per reference
  • Timeout → pending, with Check status and an automatic check on open
  • A compliance record of what was shown and accepted, kept 6 years
  • Fake bank with a visible one-shot steering control

Later slices (forward constraints only)

  • Slice 2: minimum and chosen-amount payments
  • Slice 3: cash draw from the card, fee shown before confirming
  • Slice 4: transfer history and notifications

Out of scope

  • Interest, statements, purchases checking the available limit
  • Recurring or third-party transfers, multi-currency
  • Login (the actor comes from the card)

Why the hard part is hard. 001 protects each card with a 5-second database lock (ADR-002). A bank can take up to 10 seconds to answer, or never answer. Holding the card lock across the bank call would block every purchase on that card. So this design calls the bank outside the lock and uses a pending payment to guard the gap.

02 · Architecture

Components and seams

There are no new projects, hosted services or dependencies (DEC-2). Payments are new folders in the existing three projects. The fake bank lives in Infrastructure/FakeBank with its own Bank* tables, which are never joined with card tables, so it behaves like an external system.

03 · The main mechanism

Confirm in three steps, bank outside the lock

This is DEC-3 / ADR-006, the choice everything else rests on. The figure compares it with the rejected alternative of holding the card lock across the bank call.

  1. Lock #1: load the quote and any pending payment, sum approved purchases and completed payments, and run ConfirmRules.Decide. On Create, insert Payment(Pending) with a ConfirmCreated event, mark the quote Confirmed, and commit.
  2. Unlocked: RequestDebitAsync(ref, amount) with a 10-second budget token. The fake bank is idempotent on the reference.
  3. Lock #2: recompute the sums for balance before/after, then settle Completed or Failed. If the bank didn't answer, append BankUnanswered and leave the payment Pending (receipt W-5).

A client disconnect doesn't count as "unanswered". Only the budget token decides that, and lock #2 runs with CancellationToken.None. If lock #2 times out, it retries once after 500 ms; after that the payment stays Pending and the next check settles it.

05 · Edge paths

The other flows

Bank silent, then Check status

The budget expires and the payment stays pending with W-5. Check status (the button, or the SPA automatically when it sees a pending payment, DEC-13) calls GetDebitStatusAsync outside the lock. Then:

  • Completed → effective time = bank debit time, or the settle time if the bank gives none (AC-38)
  • Refused / not received → Failed
  • Still silent → stays pending, W-10

Nothing is ever re-sent.

Balance moved since review

A purchase landed between review and confirm. Lock #1 sees owed ≠ quote amount, refuses the quote, records the event and returns W-6 with the new balance. There is no bank call. Confirming that reference again gets the same refusal (AC-30).

Two confirms at once

Both pass through lock #1 one after the other. The first registers its bank call in InFlightPayments. The second logs ConfirmRepeated, awaits that same task and returns the identical receipt. There is exactly one bank call. After a restart, it returns the stored receipt instead.

Crash between steps

The payment is Pending with no bank event. The next view open triggers the check. The bank says not received (it never got the request) or completed (it got and applied it). Both sides agree either way.

Purchase while pending (AC-36)

Pending £150.50, then a £20.00 purchase is approved, then the bank completes. The balance owed is £20.00. The record shows £150.50 settled, with £170.50 before and £20.00 after.

Fake bank's own debit

The fake bank takes its own bank:{account} lock. A known reference returns the stored outcome. Otherwise it reads and clears the steering setting, refuses if the amount exceeds the available balance, and writes the ledger. "Timeout, debit applied" writes and then hangs; "timeout, not received" writes nothing and hangs.

06 · The screen compliance cares about

Review screen (W-1, wording v1)

This is one page holding the lines and the button, with no link, tab or step between them, and no fake-bank control (AC-6, AC-33). The server renders the strings and the record stores the same text with version v1. Below is an example for the seeded Ops van card with a £150.50 balance.

Receipt and outcome messages W-2..W-10 follow one template: what happened, whether money moved, the balance now, and what to do next. Pages: cardholder view /cardholder/:id → review /cardholder/:id/pay → receipt /cardholder/:id/payments/:ref (reloadable). The admin gets a balance, an Activity table, a Payments list and a read-only record page.

10 · How to use

Run it and try every outcome

dotnet run --project src/CardLab.Api -- --webroot ../../web/dist   # local host + SPA
dotnet test tests/CardLab.Domain.Tests && npm test --prefix web -- --run   # unit
dotnet test tests/CardLab.Integration.Tests                         # integration (SQL)
npm run e2e --prefix tests/e2e                                       # E2E (smoke: -- --project smoke)
  1. Open the admin card list and follow Cardholder view on a card.
  2. In the Fake bank (simulation control) section at the bottom, pick the next outcome and press Set. It applies once, then reverts.
  3. Press Pay full balance, read the review screen and confirm. The receipt shows the outcome; pending payments offer Check status.
  4. As admin, open the card's Payments list and a record to see what was shown, who accepted it, and every event. Outcomes from steering carry the simulated flag, and deploy-smoke payments carry an origin: smoke badge.

Config: CardLab:Payments:BankTimeoutSeconds (default 10; integration tests use 1), CardLab:Payments:SettleLockRetryMs (500), CardLab:Seed:LinkedAccount:*. No new secrets or Azure resources.

12 · Risks and open items

What could go wrong, and what still needs your call

Your decision at this gate (DEC-15): marking smoke records adds a record field Origin (cardholder_view / deploy_smoke) that the approved requirements (R-14, §8) don't list. You can either:

  • (a) accept it as a one-line erratum in the requirements decisions log when the gate closes. This is the designer's recommendation: the change is additive and compliance-neutral, and it adds to the record rather than taking anything away.
  • (b) reopen the requirements for an amendment before approving the design.

13 · Terms

Glossary

Balance owed
Approved purchases minus completed payments on one card, never below £0.00.
Linked current account
The business's one account at an external bank, used to pay cards (seeded: ending 7731, £25,000.00).
Quote
What the review screen showed, stored when the review opens. It carries the reference and never changes.
Reference
PAY-XXXXXXXX. It is the idempotency key: single-use and scoped to its card.
Pending
The bank's answer isn't known yet. It blocks new payments on the card and never counts toward the balance.
Check status
Asks the bank about a reference without re-sending it. It runs from the button or automatically on page open.
Steering
A one-shot setting on the fake bank that picks the next debit or status-check outcome.
Simulated
A flag on bank outcomes produced by steering.
Origin
cardholder_view or deploy_smoke, which marks payments made by the deploy check (DEC-15).
Wording v1
The approved customer strings W-1..W-10, rendered on the server and stored verbatim.
Card lock
001's per-card database lock (sp_getapplock, 5 s timeout), held only for local steps.
R2
The risk class that requires PM and compliance-partner approval: this is the first money movement, with confirmation wording and records.
